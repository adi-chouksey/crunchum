What is freeze-drying?

How our fruit gets that signature crunch

What is freeze-drying?

Freeze-drying (also called lyophilisation) removes the water from food without cooking it. Fresh fruit is frozen, then placed in a vacuum chamber where the ice turns directly into vapour, skipping the liquid stage. Because there is no heat, the fruit keeps its shape, colour and flavour.

Step by step

  • Flash frozen: the fruit is frozen solid at peak ripeness to preserve its cellular structure.
  • Cold vacuum: under low pressure the ice sublimates straight into vapour, skipping flavour-killing heat.
  • Goodness kept: fibre, colour and natural flavour stay with the fruit.
  • The crunch: with the moisture removed, the fruit becomes a light, crisp, intensely flavourful snack.

Why not regular dried fruit?

Traditional dried fruit is dried with heat. It becomes chewy, darker and often gets sugar syrup or oil added. Freeze-dried fruit stays crunchy and light, and ours is a single ingredient: the fruit.

How to enjoy it

Snack it straight from the resealable pouch, or add a handful to yoghurt, kwark, overnight oats, smoothie bowls, muesli or your baking.
