What is freeze-drying?

Cold vacuum

Under a vacuum, ice turns straight into vapour, skipping flavour-killing heat.

Fibre, colour and natural flavour stay with the fruit.

FAQs

Good questions

What are freeze-dried fruits?

Fruit that is frozen and then gently dried under a cold vacuum, so the ice turns straight into vapour. The result is light, crunchy fruit that keeps its shape, colour and flavour without added sugar or preservatives.

Are freeze-dried fruits healthy?

Our freeze-dried fruit is just fruit with the water removed, so it keeps the fibre and natural sugars of the fresh fruit. Because the water is gone, it is more concentrated per gram, so a handful goes a long way.

Do Crunchum fruit snacks contain added sugar?

No. Each pack contains a single ingredient: the fruit. There is no added sugar, no preservatives and nothing fried. The sweetness comes from the fruit itself.

How is freeze-dried fruit different from regular dried fruit?

Regular dried fruit is dried with heat, which makes it chewy and often comes with added sugar or oil. Freeze-drying uses cold and a vacuum instead, so the fruit stays crunchy and keeps more of its fresh colour and flavour.
